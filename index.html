A Week Inside My Bento Box

Exploring how food, emotion, and social habits shape everyday health.

Why Bento Boxes?

Every meal carries more than nutrition. It carries emotion, routine, comfort, stress, and social connection.

A Bento Box naturally separates food into different spaces, making it similar to the structure of data visualisation. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner become compartments of information, where colours, shapes, and scores reveal patterns of health and emotion.

Explore the Week

Each Bento Box represents one day. Shapes, colours, and scores reveal eating behaviour, emotional states, and food balance.

What Did the Data Reveal?

Emotional Eating

Days with lower moods often included more fried food and desserts.

Social Meals

Meals shared with more people were usually rated as more enjoyable.

Vegetable Intake

Vegetables appeared less frequently during stressful weekdays.

Dinner Patterns

Dinner consistently had the highest health score across the week.
