## Final Refinement and Development

As the project is approaching completion, this week focused on the final refinement stage. At this point, my goal was not only to improve the visual and interactive aspects of the project, but also to carefully reflect on the feedback received during studio consultations and make final adjustments before submission.

After receiving feedback last week, I identified an important challenge: how to transform viewers from observers into participants. I wanted the project to have a greater impact on audiences rather than simply asking them to look at my personal data. To address this, I continued developing the interactive features introduced in the previous week, including the new p5.js component that allows users to engage with the Bento Box system themselves.

Since the interaction had already improved significantly, I decided to focus on making the content clearer and more accessible. My main goal was to help audiences understand the visual language of the project more easily. To achieve this, I created a visual guide using Figma that explains the meaning of the Bento Box elements, including shapes, colours, and health scores. This acts like a menu or legend, allowing viewers to understand the visualisation before exploring the data.

I also expanded the written content throughout the website. New explanatory text was added to describe how the original food diary was transformed into data and how the Bento Box system communicates patterns of health, food choices, and emotions. These additions help viewers who may not be familiar with data visualisation understand the project more easily.

Another important improvement was strengthening the narrative structure of the website. Instead of simply presenting data, the website now guides viewers through the process from data collection, to data translation, to visualisation, and finally to reflection. This creates a clearer story and helps audiences understand the reasoning behind my design decisions.

Finally, I created a food diary section to show the original data before it was transformed into the Bento Box visualisation. To maintain a consistent visual style across the website, I used AI image editing tools to refine the diary page and present it in a cleaner and more professional format. This helps connect the raw data with the final visual outcome while keeping the overall presentation visually consistent. I also added pagination, where more exquisite bento boxes can be generated, along with more detailed health advice.Users can also save it as an image. Enhance the user experience.

## Project Statement
**Inside The Bento Box**

Inside My Bento Box is a project that looks at how the food we eat's connected to our feelings and our health. It takes the information from a food diary. Turns it into pictures and colors like a Bento Box. The project uses shapes and colors to show us things about our habits that we might not notice otherwise.

You can see the project on a website that takes you through the process. There is a part where you can see the food diary and another part that explains what the different shapes and colors mean. Then you can see how all this information is put together in a Bento Box picture. You can look at how the food we eat is connected to how we feel and our health.

This project is not about looking at sets of data or using special devices to track our health. It is about how we can use the information from our lives to understand ourselves better. It is also about thinking about how we turn our experiences into pictures and numbers. Things like how we feel and what we taste are complicated and hard to put into words. We have to simplify them when we turn them into pictures. The Bento Box is a way to think about what we gain and what we lose when we turn our experiences into data.

The website also has a part where you can make your Bento Box. You can choose how you are feeling and what you ate and see how these things affect your health. This project is trying to get people involved and thinking about their habits rather than just looking at the project. By combining our information, stories and interactions Inside My Bento Box is asking us to think about food in a new way. Not just as something that gives us energy but, as something that is connected to our feelings and our daily lives.
